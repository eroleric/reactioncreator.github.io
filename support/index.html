01
Frame + source

Set up the reaction.

How do I start a reaction?+

Choose 9:16, PiP, Cutout, or 16:9, add the source video, position the frame, and tap to record your reaction.

Which layout should I choose?+

Use 9:16 for vertical Shorts and Reels, 16:9 for wide video, PiP when you want a movable camera window, and Cutout when you want the creator placed directly over the source.

Can I adjust the reaction frame?+

Yes. Adjust the framing, swap the source and camera positions, mirror the camera, or change the setup before recording.

02
Source + camera

Capture the take.

How do the recording controls work?+

The controls let you pause recording, pause the source, swap views, adjust framing, mirror the camera, or change focus during the take.

Why did the source or camera stop?+

Check that Reaction Creator has camera, microphone, and media permissions, close other apps using the camera, then reopen the source and try the take again.

Do I have to use Editor Mode?+

No. A good recording can go directly to export. Editor Mode is optional when you want to trim, add text, adjust audio, or use effects.

03
Finish + find

Export the reaction.

Why can an export take time?+

Longer clips, higher-resolution media, and more complex compositions can take additional processing time on the device. Keep the app open until the export finishes.

Where can I find a finished reaction?+

Open Export History to find completed exports. Saved or unfinished work remains available from Projects.

What are the export limits?+

Free includes 3 daily exports, Premium Monthly includes 15 daily exports, and Premium Annual includes unlimited exports. Compare plans.

04
Plan + privacy

Manage the account.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?+

Use Google Play subscriptions from the same Google account used for the purchase to view, manage, or cancel a plan.

How do I delete my account?+

Use the account deletion option in the app, or follow the steps on the Account Deletion page if you cannot access it.

Where can I read the privacy information?+

The Privacy Policy explains how Reaction Creator handles app and account information.
