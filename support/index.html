01

Recording & layouts

How do I start a reaction?

Choose 9:16, PiP, Cutout, or 16:9, add the source video, position the frame, and tap to record your reaction.

Can I adjust the reaction frame?

Yes. Use the recording controls to adjust the framing, swap the source and camera positions, mirror the camera, or change the setup before recording.

Do I have to use Editor Mode?

No. A good recording can go directly to export. Editor Mode is optional when you want to trim, add text, adjust audio, or use effects.

02

Exports & projects

Why can an export take time?

Longer clips and more complex video composition can take additional time to process on the device.

Where can I find a finished reaction?

Open Export History to find completed exports. Saved or unfinished work remains available from your projects.

Can I edit something after recording?

Yes. Open Editor Mode for a recent recording, saved project, export, or imported video when it needs more polish.

03

Plans & billing

What are the export limits?

The Free plan includes 3 daily exports, Premium Monthly includes 15 daily exports, and Premium Annual includes unlimited exports.

How do I manage a subscription?

Use Google Play subscriptions from the same Google account used for the purchase to manage or cancel a plan.

Where can I compare the plans?

Visit the Pricing page for the complete Free, Premium Monthly, and Premium Annual comparison.
