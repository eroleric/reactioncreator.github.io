Common questions

Quick answers

How do I start a reaction?

Import an original video, choose a reaction source, then record.

Why can export take time?

Longer clips and local composition can take more time to render.

Can I change the layout?

Yes. Pick the layout before exporting and adjust the framing as needed.

How do I manage a subscription?

Use Google Play subscriptions from the same Google account used for the purchase.
