Account Deletion

Last updated: June 16, 2026. Reaction Creator is published by Phantom Code. This page explains how to request deletion of a Reaction Creator account.

What deletion means Delete in the app Without the app Timing Data deleted Data retained Subscriptions Exports and sharing Contact

What account deletion means

Deleting a Reaction Creator account starts a pending deletion period of up to 24 hours. After that period, the scheduled backend process is designed to delete or anonymize the Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore account data according to the implemented deletion process.

Deleting the app from your phone is not the same as deleting your account. Uninstalling the app removes app access from that device, but it does not send an account deletion request to Reaction Creator.

Account deletion does not cancel Google Play subscriptions and does not automatically remove local files, public exports, shared copies, Google Play purchase records, RevenueCat records, Firebase Analytics history, Crashlytics reports, Cloud Functions logs, or other operational logs.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Reaction Creator.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Open the side menu.
  4. Find the account removal section.
  5. Tap Delete account.
  6. Confirm the deletion request.

After the request is accepted, Reaction Creator signs you out and marks the account as pending deletion.

Request deletion without the app

If you cannot access the app, email reactioncreatorteam@gmail.com.

Include the subject line Reaction Creator account deletion request, the Google account email address used to sign in to Reaction Creator, and a short statement that you want to delete your Reaction Creator account.

For your security, support may need to verify that you control the account before acting on a request.

How long deletion takes

The in-app deletion request starts a pending deletion period of up to 24 hours.

If you sign in again before the cancellation period expires, the deletion request is cancelled and the account remains active.

After the pending deletion period expires, signing in no longer reactivates the account. A scheduled backend process runs periodically and is designed to delete or anonymize the Firebase Authentication account and associated Firestore account data according to the implemented deletion process.

Because the backend process runs on a schedule, final processing can occur after the pending period has ended.

Data handled by the app-account deletion process

The implemented backend process is designed to delete or anonymize:

  • the Firebase Authentication account for Reaction Creator
  • the associated Firestore account document
  • associated nested Firestore quota reservation records

Data that may be retained

The account deletion process does not automatically remove:

  • RevenueCat customer records or subscription entitlement history
  • Google Play purchase or subscription records
  • Firebase Analytics history, aggregates, or reports
  • Firebase Crashlytics crash reports and diagnostics
  • Cloud Functions, Cloud Logging, or other operational logs
  • app-private media, projects, settings, preferences, or export files that remain on your device
  • public Gallery, Movies, or Downloads exports
  • copies that you shared to another app or service
  • records required for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, security, or support purposes

To remove device files, use Reaction Creator delete actions where available, clear app storage, uninstall the app, or delete public exports from your device Gallery or Files app.

Subscriptions and purchases

Deleting a Reaction Creator account does not cancel a Google Play subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

Google Play and RevenueCat may keep purchase and subscription records after the Reaction Creator account is deleted. These records are handled under their policies and legal requirements.

You may contact reactioncreatorteam@gmail.com to request deletion or removal of associated RevenueCat customer data where supported. Reaction Creator cannot delete Google Play billing records.

Exported and shared files

Recorder exports saved inside Reaction Creator are app-private until you download or share them.

Recorder exports downloaded to public Downloads and Editor Mode exports copied to public Movies storage remain on your device until you delete them.

When you share an export with another app, the receiving app controls its copy. Deleting your Reaction Creator account does not delete files from the receiving app.

Analytics, crash, ad, and subscription records

Firebase Analytics aggregate data, Crashlytics diagnostic records, Google Mobile Ads and UMP records, RevenueCat customer or subscription records, Google Play purchase records, and Google Cloud logging or security records may remain according to provider retention rules or user control. These records are not automatically removed by the in-app account deletion worker.

Contact

Reaction Creator is published by Phantom Code. For account deletion questions, privacy questions, or requests that are not completed through the app, email reactioncreatorteam@gmail.com.
