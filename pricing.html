Plan questions

Before you choose.

Clear answers before you start creating.

Where do I download Reaction Creator?

Reaction Creator is available for Android through Google Play. Use the download button below to open the app listing.

Do I have to edit every reaction?

No. You can record and export a good take directly. Editor Mode is available only when you want to refine it.

What does unlimited exports mean?

The Premium Annual plan does not have the daily export limit used by the Free and Premium Monthly plans.

Where will I manage a paid plan?

Paid plans are purchased and managed through Google Play.
