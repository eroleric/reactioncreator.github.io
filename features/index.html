What is Reaction Creator?+

Reaction Creator is an Android reaction video maker built around one focused flow: choose a layout, add a source, record the source and facecam together, then export.

Which reaction video layouts are supported?+

Choose 9:16 vertical, 16:9 landscape, picture-in-picture, or Cutout for Shorts, Reels, TikTok, YouTube reactions, gaming, memes, and commentary.

Do I have to edit every recording?+

No. A good take can move directly to export. Editor Mode is there when the reaction needs trimming, text, audio adjustments, or effects.
