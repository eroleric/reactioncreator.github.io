Overview

ReactionCreator helps users create reaction videos. This Privacy Policy explains the types of information that may be collected when people use the app, website, or support channels.

Information we collect

  • Account information, such as sign-in status, email address, display name, and account plan when users choose to sign in.
  • Usage and export information, such as daily export counts and app status needed to enforce plan limits.
  • Billing status handled through Google Play and subscription providers. Full payment details are not collected by this website.
  • Diagnostics, crash reports, analytics events, and advertising signals where enabled in the app.
  • Support information that users choose to send by email, including device and app details in bug reports.

How we use information

  • To provide app features, account access, export limits, premium access, and saved export workflows.
  • To respond to support requests, bug reports, suggestions, and partnership inquiries.
  • To improve reliability, understand feature usage, reduce abuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • To manage subscriptions, restore purchases, and confirm plan status.

Third-party services

The app may use services such as Firebase, Google sign-in, Google Play billing, RevenueCat, AdMob, and crash or analytics tooling. These providers may process information according to their own policies.

Your choices

  • Users can choose whether to sign in with Google.
  • Users can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play.
  • Signed-in users can request account deletion from the app's help area or by contacting support.
  • Users can choose what information to include in support emails.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact reactioncreatorteam@gmail.com.
